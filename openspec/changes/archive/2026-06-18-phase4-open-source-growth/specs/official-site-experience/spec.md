## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Official site communicates value in the first screen
The official site SHALL present a clear value proposition, a strong call to action, and immediate evidence of what Golem does above the fold.

#### Scenario: Visitor lands on homepage
- **WHEN** a first-time visitor opens the homepage
- **THEN** they SHALL see what Golem is, why it is different, and what action to take next without scrolling deeply

### Requirement: Official site supports bilingual navigation
The official site SHALL support both English and Simplified Chinese navigation for primary marketing and documentation entrypoints.

#### Scenario: User switches language
- **WHEN** a visitor changes site language
- **THEN** the site SHALL preserve equivalent content structure and navigation between supported languages

### Requirement: Official site includes conversion assets
The official site SHALL include demo media, documentation links, benchmark or proof sections, and GitHub conversion links.

#### Scenario: Visitor evaluates credibility
- **WHEN** a visitor explores the homepage or proof pages
- **THEN** they SHALL find screenshots, demos, benchmark/showcase evidence, and direct GitHub entrypoints
